What the texts fix, and what they do not. The gathering, the sun, the search for an intercessor, the following of what each worshipped, the seeing, the bridge and the qantarah are linked in sequence by the narrations. The Pool, the books, the reckoning and the scale are certain, but the texts used here do not fix their order relative to each other or to the bridge. Whether the Pool is before or after the bridge is a well known question; this site does not decide it and only shows what the texts say.
